How the choice is made

{{ paintingCount }} paintings are divided into {{ colourCount }} colour groups of {{ smallestGroup }} to {{ largestGroup }}, and no painting belongs to more than one. You are offered {{ shownCount }} of the colours, drawn at random. Choose one and a painting from its group is picked at random, never the one already on the wall. There is no model and no profile, and nothing about your choice is stored. The groupings and the readings are one person's interpretation; the facts on each label link to their source.
